# Taxonomy prefix names vs. the official SCNS handbook

Source: `SCNS 2023-2024 Handbook Final_MAR 2024.pdf` (FLDOE, March 2024), the discipline-grouped
prefix listing. Titles are taken verbatim from the handbook and converted from its ALL-CAPS style
to title case.

**388 of 597 prefix names differ** from the handbook. Most are pre-existing approximations,
not names from the earlier round of corrections.

Your `EMC` / `EML` edit matches the handbook exactly, which is how this source was identified as
the one to defer to.

**How to use:** scan the `Proposed (SCNS)` column and strike anything you disagree with. Rows are
grouped by whether the change is cosmetic or substantive.
