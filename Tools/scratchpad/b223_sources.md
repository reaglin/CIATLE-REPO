### ⚠⚠⚠ THE STRONGEST MISFILING EVIDENCE YET: one institution carrying BOTH numbers and filing them correctly

**`INR3503` is statewide *Model United Nations*. FAMU matches. UWF teaches *International Organizations*
on it.** ⚠ **What makes this decisive rather than a judgement call is a new kind of evidence.**

**The three independent tests all fire:**

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| **Title/description test** (batch 208) | ✅ **AGREE** — statewide title says Model UN and the statewide description says *"prepares students to represent an assigned country in a national Model United Nations."* **So the state is the reference and a deviating carrier is misfiling.** |
| **Dedicated-number tell** (batch 203) | ✅ **Florida numbers International Organizations separately — TWICE**: `INR3502` (FAMU, FIU, FAU, FSU, UF) and `INR4502` (USF, UCF, FGCU). ⚠ **Nine public institutions across two numbers.** |
| ⚠⚠⚠ **NEW: the same-institution control** | ✅✅ **FAMU carries BOTH `INR3502` AND `INR3503`, and files them correctly** — International Organizations on one, Model UN on the other. |

⚠⚠⚠ **That third test is worth promoting to a standing diagnostic, because it settles the question in a
way nothing else does.** Every previous misfiling case rested on *inference*: the state says X, a
dedicated number for Y exists, therefore the carrier teaching Y on X's number is misfiling. ⚠ **A
sceptic could always answer that the two subjects are not really distinct, or that the dedicated number
is dormant.**

> **When ONE institution carries BOTH numbers and files them the way the state does, both objections
> close.** **It proves the two subjects are distinct in practice and that the state's assignment is
> workable — because somebody is working it.**

⚠ **Cheap to check: on finding a suspected misfiling, look for an institution that carries the
suspected-correct number AND the number in question.** **Here FAMU was already in the carrier list for
both.**

### ⚠ FSU parsing: the register's own caution fired, in a new way

**`grep` for `INR 4124` in FSU's political science bulletin returned nothing, although the entry is
there.** ⚠ **The markup splits the identifier across two `<strong>` tags** — `<strong>INR </strong>` then
`<strong>4124. </strong>` — **so no contiguous string `INR 4124` exists in the HTML at all.**

✅ **Recovered by stripping tags and collapsing whitespace before searching.** ⚠ **The register already
warns that a number followed by a period is not enough to locate an FSU entry; this adds that the
identifier may not be contiguous in the source. Strip tags first, then search.**
