### ⚠⚠⚠ HEADLINE — `PHY3106` is a sequence-POSITION divergence, and the prerequisite proves it

| | Statewide / FIU | UWF |
|---|---|---|
| **Title** | *Modern Physics I* / *Modern Physics* | ⚠⚠ ***Calculus-Based Physics III*** |
| **Content** | "special relativity, wave-particle duality, origins of quantum mechanics, and the **Schrödinger wave equation**" | "laws of **thermodynamics**, **wave phenomena**, breakdown of classical physics, theory of relativity, quantization of charge, light, and energy, atomic structure" |
| **Gate** | `PHY 2049`/`2054` **AND `MAC 2313`** (Calculus III) | ⚠ **`PHY 2049` only** |
| **Position** | **above** the introductory sequence | ⚠⚠ **the third term OF it** |

⚠⚠⚠ **The content diverges at both ends.** UWF adds thermodynamics and wave phenomena — classical
material that closes an introductory sequence — and does not mention quantum mechanics. FIU reaches the
Schrödinger equation. **Only relativity overlaps.**

⚠⚠ **And the prerequisite settles the position in one line, which is the reusable part:** a course
sitting *inside* an introductory sequence cannot require mathematics that sequence has not reached yet.
**So where two carriers disagree about level, compare the gates — the one demanding later mathematics is
the later course.** Recorded as the prerequisite-as-signal diagnostic firing on **position**, alongside
subject (175), depth (185) and emphasis (187).

⚠ **This completes a pair with the live `PHY3107` guide (batch 204)** and confirms its sequence-LENGTH
finding from the other side: **`PHY3101` *Elements of Modern Physics* covers the whole field in ONE term
and has TEN public carriers against 2 for the `3106`/`3107` pair.** **The one-term treatment is five
times commoner than the sequence**, so a student completing part 1 should check that part 2 exists
anywhere they are heading.

### ⚠⚠⚠ `PHY4445` — a statewide prerequisite naming two numbers NOBODY carries

**The statewide gate reads `PHY 3054 OR PHY 3049`. Checked both: ZERO public carriers in Florida for
either.** They are well-formed SCNS identifiers, so they pass the batch-209 syntax test — **they simply
name nothing anyone can enrol in.**

⚠⚠ **This is the extreme of the dangling shape and it is worth adding to the taxonomy as its own row.**
Batch 219's `TPA4021C` named a number that belonged to *other* schools; **this names two that belong to
nobody.** **So run the carrier count on every token, not only against the carrier you are writing
about** — a zero means the gate cannot be satisfied by any student in the state.

⚠ **The real gate at UWF is `MAC 2313` AND `MAP 2302` AND `PHY 2049`, C− or better in all.**
**Differential equations is the one to flag** — nothing in the state record hints at it, and laser rate
equations and cavity mode analysis are differential-equation problems. The batch-189 shape.

**And a scope divergence with an institutional explanation:** UWF carries the statewide syllabus in full
— a broad **photonics survey** (fibre optics, photodetectors, modulation, displays, optical
communications). **UCF's is *"principles of laser gain media, properties of resonators and modes"*** —
narrower and deeper, laser physics proper. ⚠⚠ **UCF hosts the College of Optics and Photonics, so its
applications material lives in its own courses.** **Written as a genuine choice for the student — breadth
against depth — rather than as a defect**, with a note that the narrower version is the stronger
preparation for optics graduate study.

### ⚠⚠⚠ `PHY4822L` — the credit divergence is PACKAGING, not depth

| | UWF | Florida State |
|---|---|---|
| Credits | **3** | **2** |
| Repeatable? | ⚠ **No** | ⚠⚠ **Yes — to 6 credit hours**, *"for special projects arranged in advance"* |
| Catalogued as | `PHY4822L` | ⚠ **`PHY 4822Lr`** — the `r` marks repeatability |
| Gate | `PHY 3107` **AND** `PHY 3802L` | `PHY 3802L` |

⚠⚠⚠ **The institution with fewer credits per enrolment offers more credit in total, and the better route
into a graduate application.** FSU runs a smaller repeatable unit a committed student takes three times;
UWF runs one larger block and stops.

**Recorded as a rule: a lower credit value is not evidence of a shallower course until you have checked
whether it repeats.** ⚠ The tell is cheap — a repeat clause in the credit line, or a suffix convention
like FSU's `r`.

⚠⚠⚠ **And the best sentence in the batch, from FSU's catalogue: *"Students are expected to work without
detailed instructions."*** That is the real discontinuity in a physics degree — every earlier laboratory
supplies a procedure; this one supplies apparatus, a question and a deadline. **The guide leads on it,
because it is what the course is FOR and what graduate committees and employers actually read it for.**

⚠ Two further notes: **an `L` suffix at 2–3 credits standing alone** — the batch-203 counterexample to
"`L` means a 1-credit companion", stated in the guide; and **`PHY4823L` (Modern Physics Laboratory II)
exists at USF alone**, so a second lab should not be assumed.

### ⚠ Contact hours: the laboratory convention, and a warning correct to keep

**`PHY4822L` published at 90 contact hours for the 3-credit form** — roughly **30 hours per credit**, the
laboratory convention stated in this file, **not** the 1:15 classroom convention which misdescribes a
standalone lab badly. FSU's 2-credit form is about 60.

⚠ **`validate_drafts.py` warns** (*"3 credits with 90 contact hours (expected ~45, or ~60 for a C
course)"*). **That warning is correct to keep**, the same class as the flight-hour and clinical-practicum
cases already documented: the lecture heuristic does not model a standalone laboratory. **The derivation
is stated in the guide.**
