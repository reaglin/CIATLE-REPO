### ⚠⚠⚠ WHY THESE TWO WERE REQUESTED — and the predictor it gives us

**Both requests are the `L` half of a number whose integrated `C` twin ALREADY HAD A LIVE GUIDE**
(`BSC1010C` and `BSC1020C` were published earlier in the project). **The requester found the site's page
for the packaging their institution does not use, and needed the other one.**

⚠⚠ **That makes a published `C` guide with no `L` guide — or the reverse — a PREDICTOR of a future
request**, and checking for the sibling is one lookup. **Writing both halves at once costs far less than
meeting the request later**, because the second guide reuses the entire survey. Recorded as a standing
check in `CLAUDE.md`.

### ⚠⚠⚠ The student-facing headline, and it is PROVED rather than asserted

**The lab is a separate registration, and it is what makes the subject a laboratory science.** The
3-credit lecture alone does not satisfy a "science with laboratory" requirement.

**The proof comes from Indian River State College's catalogue** — its *Microbiology for Health Sciences*
(`MCB2010`), the gateway course for nursing and allied health, requires:

> *"BSC 1020 and BSC 1020L, or BSC 2010 and BSC 2010L, or BSC 2085 and BSC 2085L, all with grade of C or
> higher."*

⚠⚠ **Every one of the three accepted routes names a lecture AND its laboratory.** A student holding only
`BSC1020` is blocked. ✅ **And the good news is equally worth stating: the NON-MAJORS route is accepted as
an equal entry** alongside majors biology and anatomy &amp; physiology.

**This is the prerequisite-as-signal diagnostic (batches 175/185) used to establish a course's FUNCTION
rather than its subject — and it is the single most useful sentence in either guide.**

### ⚠⚠ The two carriers of `BSC1020L` protect the student differently

| | Indian River | Gulf Coast |
|---|---|---|
| Relationship to lecture | ⚠⚠ **reciprocal COREQUISITE** — neither can be taken alone | ⚠ **no corequisite**; *"recommended for students with the requirement of a science laboratory"* |
| Published hours | not published | ✅ **2 lab hours weekly** |
| Lab fee | not published | ✅ **$47.00**, fall and spring only |
| Gen-ed designation on the LAB | ✅ natural science | ❌ none |

⚠⚠⚠ **Same number, opposite protection against the very mistake the number is known for.** At Indian
River the pairing is enforced; at Gulf Coast nothing stops a student registering for the lecture alone and
discovering later that they needed the lab.

### ⚠⚠ The `hs_credit` INVERSION — the easier course earns the science credit

| Family | Dual-enrolment high-school credit |
|---|---|
| **`BSC1020` / `BSC1020L`** — non-majors human biology | ✅ **SCIENCE** |
| `BSC1010` / `BSC1010L` / `BSC1010C` — majors | ⚠ **ELECTIVE** |
| `BSC2010` family — majors | ⚠ **ELECTIVE** |

⚠⚠⚠ **The HARDER majors laboratory returns an elective; the EASIER non-majors one fills a high-school
science requirement.** Both guides state it with *"confirm with your counsellor and your district's
articulation agreement."*

⚠ **This is the batch-227 sibling-comparison rule again: the finding came from comparing family members,
not from a prefix distribution.** The distribution decides whether to look; the siblings say what to write.

### ⚠⚠ `(GE CORE)` is on the number — but the LAB'S designation is institutional

The statewide title for century `010` carries **`(GE CORE)`**, the batch-202 strongest-transfer-fact
marker. ⚠⚠ **The batch-202 caution bites exactly here: the protection attaches to the AREA, not to a
laboratory component** — and the data agrees. **Only 4 of 7 `BSC1010L` carriers record a natural-science
designation on the LAB** (FAMU, FAU, FSWSC, PBSC yes; CFK, PESC, SSCF no).

**The guide says: do not assume the laboratory inherits its lecture's status; ask which requirement it
fills at your institution.**

### ⚠ Three anomalies, recorded rather than explained away

- ⚠⚠ **Seminole State College carries `BSC1010L` but NOT `BSC1010`.** Its majors lecture is the
  integrated `BSC2010C`, which already contains a laboratory. **An orphan lab.** The guide tells Seminole
  students to ask an adviser what it is for rather than guessing at a teach-out.
  ⚠ **Note the code trap: `SSCF` is SEMINOLE STATE, not State College of Florida (`SCFMS`)** — the
  batch-215 caution, and it nearly produced a wrong institution name in the guide.
- ⚠⚠ **Valencia carries `BSC1010` and `BSC1010C` BOTH AT 4 CREDITS** — so the suffix is separating
  **honours from standard**, not lecture from integrated. **A real caution on the batch-184 diagnostic;
  recorded in `CLAUDE.md`.**
- **Gulf Coast's flat-file title for `BSC1020L` is "HUMAN BIOLOGY"** — identical to its lecture — while
  its catalogue says "Human Biology Lab". **Register by number.**

### ⚠⚠⚠ And a contact-hour correction with back-catalogue reach

**Gulf Coast publishes 3 lab hours for the MAJORS biology lab (`BSC2010L`) and 2 for the NON-MAJORS one
(`BSC1020L`) — same college, same 1 credit, a 50% difference in scheduled time.**

⚠⚠ **So "1-credit `L` = 45 hours" is a ceiling, not a default**, and the project has been applying it as
a default. **Prefer a published figure; where none exists, anchor the derivation on the closest published
comparator rather than on the bare convention** — which is what `BSC1010L`'s 45 is built on, and the guide
states the anchor.
