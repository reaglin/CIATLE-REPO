### ⚠⚠⚠ HEADLINE — `FIL4036` carries THREE different spans, and the sequence diverges TWICE

| | Statewide | Florida Atlantic | UWF |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Title** | *Film History 1* | *Film to the 1940s* | *History of Motion Pictures* |
| **Span** | 1890s → **1959** | ⚠ 1890s → **the 1940s** | ⚠⚠ **no boundary stated** |
| **Structure** | two courses | two courses | ⚠⚠ **one course; UWF carries NO `FIL4037`** |
| **Ordering** | part 2 **requires** part 1 | ⚠ *"May be taken BEFORE FIL 4036"* | n/a |
| **Credits** | — | **4** | **3** |

⚠⚠⚠ **Roughly two decades fall inside this course for the state and outside it at FAU** — film noir,
Italian neorealism, the Hollywood blacklist, the Paramount decree breaking up the studio system, the
arrival of television and the widescreen response. **A student can complete "Film History 1" at either
institution and have covered materially different material.**

**Two divergences on one sequence, not one: the BOUNDARY and the ORDERING** — plus UWF's single-course
structure, which is the batch-204 sequence-length shape. **Three structures for one subject.**

### ⚠⚠ And the batch-181 partner check paid off again

**`FIL4037` is *Film History 2* statewide and at FAU — but USF carries `FIL4037` as *HISTORY OF VIDEO
ART*.** ⚠ Video art is an avant-garde fine-art practice, not a continuation of film history.

**So a student looking for the second half cannot simply enrol in `FIL4037` wherever they find it.**
The batch-181 drill said *on finding a divergence, probe the partner* — done, and it surfaced a second,
unrelated problem the first search would never have shown.

### ⚠⚠ `FIL3833` — a title divergence that DISSOLVES, and the conjunction test that PASSES

**UNF calls it *Film Genre*; UWF calls it *Film Styles*; statewide is *Film Styles*.** Genre and style
are genuinely different critical categories, so this reads as a collision.

⚠ **It is not. The statewide description names BOTH** — *"the STYLES of selected filmmakers with
emphasis on GENRES, national movements."* **UWF's own description covers genres (Westerns, sci-fi,
noir), movements (German Expressionism, Soviet Montage, Italian Neo-Realism, the New Waves) AND
production approaches.** Each carrier titled the course after one of the two halves it teaches.
**Handled as a REASSURANCE per batch 227, with the transfer action attached.**

⚠⚠ **Separately, `FIL4102` gives the batch-207 conjunction test its first recorded PASS.** Statewide
title promises *"Screenwriting AND Storyboarding"*; UWF's title drops storyboarding; **its description
delivers it** (*"script formats, storyboarding, and story pitches"*). **Every prior instance of that
test was a failure, which made it look like a defect detector — recording the pass matters.**

### ⚠⚠⚠ `FIL4102` — a Gordon Rule WRITING designation, and two exclusion rules

**UWF flags `gordon_rule` + `gordon_writing`, and its catalogue carries the UWF label *"Meets
College-Level Communication Skills Requirement"*** — which the batch-179 rule identifies as the
**writing** half. ⚠⚠ **So a grade of C or higher is required for it to count**, which is stricter than
the ordinary passing standard and stricter than what shows as a pass on a transcript. Stated in the
guide, with the reminder that the designation is institutional.

⚠ **Two exclusions easy to read past:** credit may not be received for both `FIL4102` and `MMC4103`;
and the course is **repeatable to 6 sh**, worth using to leave with two finished scripts.

⚠ **The statewide prerequisite reads `INTRODUCTION TO MOTION PICTURE/`** — prose rather than an
identifier, **and truncated mid-phrase.** The batch-227 sixth shape with truncation on top.

### ⚠⚠ `FIL3427C` — institutional signature, cleanly

**USF carries `FIL3427C` *Film I*; UWF carries the bare `FIL3427` *Film Production I*. Both 3 credits,
neither carries both forms** — the batch-219 row-2 signature of one course filed two ways.

⚠ **UWF runs a three-rung ladder** — `FIL3427` → `FIL4435` Production II → `FIL4514` Production III, a
practicum ending in a finished **10–20 minute narrative short**. **The guide tells students to plan all
three from term one, because each gates the next and starting late costs a year rather than a term.**

### ⚠ The distribution test, stratified, correctly says nothing

`hs_credit` across public undergraduate `FIL`: **LOWER 54 FA / 111 EL (33% fine arts — genuinely
substantial)**; **UPPER 1 FA / 222 EL.** ⚠ **All five targets are upper division, so the field is
boilerplate for them.** The batch-227 rule applied — stratify by the population the field applies to,
and dual enrolment is a lower-division phenomenon. **One line per guide saying it says nothing.**
