### ⚠⚠⚠ EXTERNALLY-GOVERNED CURRICULUM, now with a COMMERCIAL VENDOR

**Batch 209 established the shape on `AFR` (Air Force ROTC): a body outside SCNS owns the syllabus, so
institutions cannot diverge on content, the statewide record is the LEAST current source, and the guide
should tell students to judge by what the external body issues.** ⚠⚠ **`CNT3112` is the same shape with
a VENDOR in place of a national body, and the evidence is convergent:**

| Evidence | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Title, statewide** | *Routing and Switching Essentials* — ⚠ **the earlier Cisco CCNA module 2 name** |
| **Title, UWF** | *Switching, Routing and Wireless Essentials* — ⚠ **the current CCNA module 2 name** |
| **Description difference** | UWF's is the statewide text **plus wireless** — *"routers, switches AND WIRELESS DEVICES"*, *"virtual LANs, WIRELESS LANs"* — ⚠⚠ **exactly the documented v6→v7 restructure, which folded wireless into module 2** |
| ⚠⚠⚠ **The prerequisite course** | **UWF titles `CNT3004` *"Introduction to Networks"*** — **the CCNA module 1 name** — **and NOT the statewide title (*Network Concepts*)** |

**Two consecutive courses named after two consecutive vendor modules is not coincidence.**

⚠ **Written in the guide as a strong inference with the evidence shown, not as a certainty** — no
syllabus was seen. **The actionable instruction given to the student is a question rather than a
claim: *"ask which CCNA revision this course follows"*, which also tells them which exam to sit.**

⚠⚠ **The detection method generalises and is cheap: when a course title reads like a product or
module name rather than a subject, check whether it matches a vendor certification track.** **Other
prefixes where this is likely: `CTS`, `CIS`, `CNT` and the Engineering Technology networking numbers,
all of which carry vendor-aligned coursework.**

⚠⚠⚠ **The consequence worth stating for students is a career one, and it is the opposite of the usual
transfer advice: what transfers is the CERTIFICATION, not the number.** With one public carrier the
"same course offered" condition bites hard, **but a current associate-level networking certification is
recognised by every institution and employer in the field.**

### ⚠⚠ A second PLACEHOLDER prerequisite — and a new wildcard form

**`CNT3004`'s statewide prerequisite reads `CSG X060 OR EQUIVALENT`.** ⚠ **`CSG X060` is not a valid
SCNS identifier: the `X` is a wildcard standing in for the level digit and was never replaced.**

**Second instance of the batch-222 placeholder shape after `COM4301`'s `COM 2XXX`** — ⚠⚠ **and a
different wildcard convention.** `COM 2XXX` masks the century digits and keeps the level; `CSG X060`
masks the level and keeps the century. **So the check should look for `X` anywhere in the numeric
portion, not only as a trailing mask.**

### ⚠ An institution hedging its own prerequisite, again

**`CNT4416` statewide requires `CNT4403`, `CIS4385` AND `CDA3101`.** ⚠ **UWF does not carry `CIS4385`**
(public carriers: Pensacola State, FSU), **so UWF's own prerequisite reads `(CIS 4385 OR CIS 4221) AND
CNT 4403`** — it wrote an alternative in and dropped `CDA3101`.

⚠⚠ **Same pattern as `TPA4021C` and `PLA4061`: an institution hedging its own prerequisite with an
`OR` is a reliable signal that it knows the statewide numbering does not line up at its campus.**
**Worth treating as a positive tell rather than only as a defect.**
