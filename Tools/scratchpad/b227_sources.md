## Batch 227 — GRA (graphic design), 2026-09-16

Five guides, all live: **GRA2508C**, **GRA3139C**, **GRA3881C**, **GRA3887C**, **GRA4882C** — the four
queued rows plus `GRA3887C`, which was **added** because it is the evidentiary control for the batch's
headline and it completes UWF's comics cluster. **5 clean-with-warnings, 0 blocking**; 0 of 5
prerequisites over the limit after trimming.

All five are `C` identifiers at **3 credits / 60 contact hours**. ⚠ **No Florida institution publishes a
contact-hour figure anywhere in the `GRA` prefix** — every figure in the batch is derived and labelled.

**GRA listing: 350 courses built (523 offerings), all already present — 0 created, 0 updated, 0 failed.**

**Prefix shape**: **387 live ids, 269 single-carrier (70%)**, max 11 carriers.

### ⚠⚠⚠ THE HEADLINE: a title divergence that DISSOLVES, beside one that does not

**The survey pointed hard at a misfiling finding and it was wrong.** UWF appeared to be running a comics
PAIR on two statewide numbers meaning something else — `GRA3881C` *Semantics of Design* and `GRA4882C`
*Analysis of Trends and Styles*. That is the batch-225 `PET4434`/`PET4820` shape, and it was the working
hypothesis going in.

**Reading the DESCRIPTIONS broke it in both directions.**

| | Statewide | FIU | UWF |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`GRA3881C`** title | *Semantics of Design* | *Design: Semiotics* | ⚠ *Comics: Sequential Art and Design* |
| **`GRA3881C`** description | *"the general field of **semiotics** … aspects linked to **MEANING** in visual communication"* | *"**signs, codes**, and cultural **MEANING** in design … **semiotic principles**"* | *"how **SIGNS, SYMBOLS**, and sequence are used to create **MEANING** in sequential art"* |

✅ **NOT a divergence.** All three name the same subject. **UWF teaches semiotics THROUGH comics — the
medium is the vehicle, not the subject.** The guide reassures rather than warns, and gives the student a
transfer action: an evaluator comparing those two titles will reasonably conclude they are different
courses and be wrong, so send the syllabus and point at the descriptions.

⚠ **`GRA4882C` IS a real divergence.** Statewide title and description agree with each other (an
analysis and appraisal course); UWF teaches advanced comics PRODUCTION. Branch 1 of the batch-208
title/description test, so the carrier is the deviating party.

### ⚠⚠⚠ And the explanation for `GRA4882C` is arithmetic, not carelessness — `GRA3887C` is the control

**Florida provides exactly ONE comics/cartoon number: `GRA?887`, whose statewide description explicitly
names *"COMIC STRIPS, COMIC BOOKS, GRAPHIC NOVELS."* ⚠⚠ UWF CARRIES IT, and matches the statewide title
WORD FOR WORD**, adding only a sentence about stop-motion practice.

**So UWF demonstrably knows the state's scheme and files to it when a number exists.**

| UWF course | Statewide number | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `GRA3887C` Traditional Methods in Cartoon Design | `GRA?887` — same subject, title matches exactly | ✅ **filed correctly** |
| `GRA3881C` Comics: Sequential Art and Design | `GRA?881` *Semantics of Design* | ✅ **matches in substance** |
| ⚠ `GRA4882C` Advanced Comics | `GRA?882` *Analysis of Trends and Styles* | ⚠⚠ **diverges — no number left** |

⚠⚠⚠ **The state numbers comics ONCE; UWF teaches it as a THREE-COURSE SEQUENCE. The third course has
nowhere correct to go.** That is **MISFILING BY NECESSITY (batch 225) with a NEW CAUSE** — not a subject
the state failed to number, but a **SEQUENCE LENGTH its single number cannot accommodate.**
Sequence-length divergence (batch 204) colliding with misfiling.

⚠ **Fourth instance of misfiling-by-necessity**, after `JOU4306` and the `PET4434`/`PET4820` pair — and
the first where **the number exists and the carrier already uses it.**

**Drill recorded: before concluding a carrier misfiled, count how many courses that carrier runs in the
subject against how many numbers the STATE provides.** Where the first exceeds the second, the surplus
is misfiled by arithmetic.

⚠⚠ **I nearly published the opposite of this.** The titles pointed one way and the descriptions the
other; only the batch-225 rule — read one institution's entries against each other — surfaced the
control that settled it.

### ⚠⚠⚠ A single-carrier course where statewide agreement is NOT evidence

**`GRA3887C`'s statewide description and UWF's are THE SAME TEXT.** With one carrier that is not two
sources agreeing — **it is one source quoted twice**, almost certainly contributed by UWF.

⚠ **The guide says so.** The hedging is not relaxed on the strength of it. ⚠⚠ **It does carry a mild
positive signal worth stating: the text is current and written by people who teach the course**, unlike
the 1980s entries elsewhere in the file. **The tell is verbatim agreement — genuine independent
agreement is never word-for-word.**

### ⚠⚠ A statewide prerequisite that dangles at EVERY carrier

**`GRA3881C` and `GRA4882C` both list `GRA 3194C` statewide. NEITHER UWF NOR FIU carries `GRA3194C` or
the bare `GRA3194`** — those are UF and Pensacola State, neither of which teaches either course.

⚠ **Same shape as `JOU3342` (batch 224): the gate cannot be satisfied by any carrier, and the
contributing institutions do not teach the course.** Both guides name it as a data defect and give the
real gates (FIU `GRA 2111C`; UWF none).

### ⚠⚠⚠ A prerequisite CHAIN that cannot be completed outside one institution

**`GRA4882C` requires `ART 3312C` AND `GRA 3151C` AND `GRA 3881C`.**

| Gate | Florida public carriers |
|---|---|
| `GRA3881C` | UWF, FIU |
| ⚠ `GRA3151C` | **UWF alone** |
| ⚠ `ART3312C` | **UWF alone** |

⚠⚠ **Two of the three gates exist at UWF alone, so the chain is unreproducible anywhere else in
Florida.** A transfer student cannot arrive gate-ready and will generally need to clear them at UWF
first — **which can add a term.** That is a sharper consequence than the usual single-carrier hedge, so
it went in the prerequisite field.

### ⚠⚠ A SIXTH shape of defective statewide prerequisite — PROSE instead of an identifier

**`GRA2508C`'s statewide prerequisite reads *"BASIC DESIGN OR CONSENT OF INSTRUCTOR."*** ⚠⚠ **That is a
SUBJECT DESCRIPTION, not a course identifier — and the batch-209 syntax test never fires, because there
is no course-looking token to test.**

**What it breaks: every earlier test scans course-looking TOKENS.** A prerequisite made of prose has
none, so it passes every check while telling the reader nothing actionable. **Added to the taxonomy with
the question "does this field name a COURSE at all?" placed before the token tests.** Real gate at UWF:
`DIG 2000C OR DIG 3001C`.

### ⚠⚠ The distribution test, stratified to the population the FIELD applies to

**`hs_credit` across the prefix looked marginal and would have been dropped:** 46 FINE ARTS / 462
ELECTIVE over public undergraduate rows (**9%**), which by the batch-225 threshold reads "near-universal
with exceptions."

⚠⚠ **But high-school credit only applies to DUAL ENROLMENT, which is LOWER-DIVISION.** Restricted to
levels 1-2: **43 / 267 (14%)**, with **17 of the 20 FINE ARTS numbers lower-division.**

⚠⚠⚠ **And the FINDING did not come from the distribution at all. It came from comparing SIBLINGS AT ONE
INSTITUTION: UWF carries `GRA2111C` (FINE ARTS) and `GRA2508C` (ELECTIVE) — two adjacent required
foundation courses in the SAME programme returning DIFFERENT high-school credit.** A dual-enrolled
student filling a fine-arts requirement gets it from one and not the other, and nothing they read says
so.

**The distribution test decides whether to LOOK; the sibling comparison finds what to SAY.**

### ⚠⚠ A stale statewide record PROVED rather than inferred

**`GRA2508C`'s description ends *"INSTITUTIONS: FAMU 1988"* — and FAMU does NOT carry the number today,
while UWF DOES.** ⚠ **One flat-file lookup turns the batch-208 rule from a reasonable suspicion into a
demonstrated fact**, and the guide states it as such rather than hedging.

⚠ **Its statewide TITLE also carries an administrative marker: `COLOR AND COLOR THEORY(RES 2008)`**,
appearing on exactly **three** `GRA` numbers, all stamped the same year. **Stripped from the course name,
described as administrative, and explicitly NOT guessed at** — unlike the `ATF` titles, where the
parenthesised content is real hour data.
